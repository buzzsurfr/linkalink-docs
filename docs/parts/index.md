# Parts Catalog

Every component family in the v1.4 Ultimate library, grouped the way the download is. Each page has plate previews from the supplied 3MFs, sizes, variants, assembly notes and the exact files.

## :material-desk: Base Mounts

| Family | Tier | What it's for |
|---|---|---|
| [C-Clamp Mounts](bases/c-clamps.md) | <span class="tier std">Standard</span> | Clamp an arm to a desk, shelf or workbench edge without drilling. Two styles, five opening sizes each. |
| [Desk Bases (Round & Foot)](bases/desk-bases.md) | <span class="tier std">Standard</span> | Free-standing footprints for light payloads, or screw-down bases for a permanent install. No clamp required. |
| [Fixed Rotating Base](bases/fixed-rotating-base.md) | <span class="tier ult">Ultimate</span> | A compact screw-down socket. The link joint rotates in it and a set screw locks it. The strongest desk-surface mount in the library. |
| [Fixed Wall Mount](bases/wall-mount.md) | <span class="tier ult">Ultimate</span> | Anchor an arm to a wall, cabinet side or stud, with a printable drilling template. |
| [Weighted Bases](bases/weighted-base.md) | <span class="tier ult">Ultimate</span> | Hollow bases you fill with ballast. Free-standing stability for bigger arms, with no clamp and no holes. |
| [Cup Holder Mount](bases/cup-holder.md) | <span class="tier ult">Ultimate</span> | A plug that drops into a car (or desk-organizer) cup holder and gives you a link joint on top. |
| [Rail System](bases/rail-system.md) | <span class="tier ult">Ultimate</span> | A screw-down track that one or more arms slide along. Great for workbenches and long desks. |

## The tool

`0. Start Here/LINK A LINK - TOOL.STL` (67.6 mm Ø, 12.7 mm thick) is a printed wrench for the threaded nuts and screw heads. It gives you the last bit of torque on a joint without chewing up the nut. Print one first.
