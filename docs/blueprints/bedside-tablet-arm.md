## Buy

* Ballast for the weighted base: dry sand, steel shot or BBs (≈ 1.5–3 kg for the Large base).
* Rubber bands to tension the clamp arms.

## Assemble

1. Print the Large Weighted Base, fill it with ballast through the plug hole, and close it with the Plug.
2. Fit the Weighted Base Link Joint with its screw and washer.
3. Assemble the Modular Link: slide the 200 mm body into Teethed Joint 1 and Teethed Joint 2, then lock both ends with the Locking Braces and the Screw/Nut Washers.
4. Chain: base → 225 mm link (near vertical) → Modular Link (reaching out) → 150 mm link (angling down) → Swivel Payload Link.
5. Assemble the Large Tensioned Clamp: base + cover, two arms, rubber bands for tension. Fix it to the swivel with its screw and washer.
6. Put the base on the floor/bedside table **on the tablet side**, pose it, and tighten from the base outward.

!!! tip "Tips"
    * The swivel lets you rotate portrait ↔ landscape without touching the arm joints.
    * Tablet too narrow for the Large clamp? Use the Medium Tensioned Clamp or the Modular T-Claw with 80 mm arms.

## Variations

* **Desk version:** swap the weighted base for a Rotating C-Clamp and drop the Modular Link.
* **Kitchen recipe arm:** see Blueprint 10 (wall mounted).
