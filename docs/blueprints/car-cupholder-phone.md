## Buy

* A MagSafe charger puck or MagSafe-compatible magnet ring. The mount is a holder, not a magnet.

## Assemble

1. Measure the inside diameter of your cup holder near the top and print the Cup Holder base that's the same size or just under (80/85/90/95 mm).
2. Attach the Base Link Joint to the cup base with its screw and washer.
3. Add the 125 mm link, then the Swivel Payload Link, then the MagSafe Mount.
4. Press-fit or glue your MagSafe puck into the mount; route the cable down the link.
5. Tighten hard. Road vibration loosens joints that are only finger-tight.

!!! tip "Tips"
    * Wrap a turn of felt or electrical tape around the cup base if it rattles.
    * Do not mount anywhere an airbag deploys, and keep it out of your sightline.

## Variations

* **Clamp-style phone holder:** Phone Mount (small) instead of MagSafe for non-MagSafe phones.
* **More freedom:** Ball Joint Link in place of the Swivel Payload Link.
