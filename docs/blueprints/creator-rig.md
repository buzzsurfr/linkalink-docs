**Print profile:** Riser + Branch: 5–6 walls, 30–40 % infill

## Buy

* 1–2× 1/4-20 bolts / thumb screws.
* Ballast for the weighted base (sand or steel shot).
* Your mic rides on the camera's cold shoe, so no extra parts are needed.

## Assemble

1. Fill and close the Large Weighted Base; fit its Base Link Joint.
2. Mount the 200 mm riser link vertically, then the 3-Way Branch Link on top of it.
3. **Camera arm** (side output of the branch): 125 mm link → 50 mm Payload Link → 1/4-20 Mount → camera.
4. **Light arm** (top output): 250 mm link angled up and back over the camera → Payload Link → Baby Pin Mount (for lights with a 5/8″ receiver) or 1/4-20 Mount.
5. Balance matters more than strength here: place the light arm so its weight sits over the base, opposite the camera.
6. Tighten riser and branch joints hardest; clip all cables down the riser.

!!! tip "Tips"
    * Top vs Bottom 3-Way Branch: these are mirror variants (which face the side branch comes off). Print the one that suits the side you want the camera on.
    * A 4-Way Branch Link adds a third output for a phone/teleprompter or a second light.

## Variations

* **Clamp-mounted:** Rotating C-Clamp instead of the weighted base frees desk space and handles more torque.
* **Two-camera:** use the 4-Way Branch with two 1/4-20 arms.
