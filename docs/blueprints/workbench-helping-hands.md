## Buy

* Wood screws to fix the rails to the bench (countersunk).
* TPU filament for the two Spring Clamp inserts.

## Assemble

1. Print three 200 mm rails (one plate in the 3MF) and screw them end-to-end along the back edge of the bench.
2. Slide on the **Locking** Base Link Joint for the work-holding arm, and the plain Base Link Joint for the viewing arm.
3. Arm 1: 150 mm → 125 mm → 50 mm Payload Link → Spring Clamp (TPU inserts in the jaws).
4. Arm 2: 150 mm → 50 mm Payload Link → Phone Mount (for reference photos/video) or a 1/4-20 Mount for a loupe or light.
5. Lock arm 1 on the rail with its screw and washer; slide arm 2 wherever you need it.

!!! tip "Tips"
    * Keep the soldering iron away from the links; PETG is better than PLA but still softens near 80 °C.
    * More rails = more arms. Print extra Base Link Joints.

## Variations

* **Tensioned clamp:** Small Tensioned Clamp in place of the Spring Clamp for flat boards.
* **Desk rail:** same idea on a desk for a mic + camera pair.
