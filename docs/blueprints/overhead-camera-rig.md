## Buy

* 1× 1/4-20 bolt or thumb screw.

## Assemble

1. Clamp the Rotating C-Clamp to the **back** edge of the desk, centred behind your work area.
2. Run the first 275 mm link straight up. A small tilt toward the desk (≈ 2°) compensates for sag.
3. Run the second 275 mm link horizontally over the desk.
4. Turn the Swivel Payload Link so its payload face points at the desk and attach the 1/4-20 Mount and camera.
5. Tighten joint 1 (base) and joint 2 (elbow) hardest. They carry the full lever arm.
6. Level the camera with the swivel, then clip the USB/HDMI cable along the links.

!!! tip "Tips"
    * Reach is ~275 mm forward of the base pivot. A 13″ desk area is easy; for a wider field, raise the camera rather than pushing it further out.
    * If the elbow droops over time, tighten the joint and consider PETG. PLA creeps under constant load.

## Variations

* **Fine leveling:** replace the Swivel Payload Link with the **Ball Joint Link**.
* **Overhead phone:** replace the 1/4-20 Mount with the MagSafe Mount.
