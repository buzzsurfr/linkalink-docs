## Where to ask what

* **Can't find your files / sign-in problems:** the Kickstarter comments or a direct message to the creator. Password-reset emails can be slow or land in spam.
* **How do I build X?** Discord first, then this guide's [Blueprints](../blueprints/index.md).
* **Share a remix:** Pro Builds › Community.
